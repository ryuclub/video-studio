## 二 简介

```
Ninety-seven years. That's the average resurfacing interval for a local road in England and Wales.

Councils filled 1.9 million potholes last year — more than 5,200 every day, at a cost of £149.3 million. The repair backlog still went up. It now stands at £18.62 billion, and would take 12 years to clear.

To be fair: maintenance budgets rose 17%, and road conditions did improve. By three points.

So you pay road tax. You pay for the pothole. And you pay for your own suspension.

Three times. One hole.

All figures: ALARM Survey 2026, Asphalt Industry Alliance, published 17 March 2026. Sources in the pinned comment.

How long has yours been there?
```

中文对照：

> 九十七年。这是英格兰与威尔士地方道路重铺一次的平均间隔。

> 地方政府去年填了 190 万个坑——每天 5,200 多个，花掉 £1.493 亿。积压反而还涨了。现在是 £186.2 亿，按当前拨款要 12 年才清得完。
>
> 公平地说：养护预算涨了 17%，路况也确实改善了。改善了三个百分点。
>
> 所以你交道路税。你出钱填坑。你还得自费换避震。
>
> 三次。一个坑。
>
> 全部数据：ALARM Survey 2026，沥青工业联盟，2026 年 3 月 17 日发布。来源见置顶评论。
