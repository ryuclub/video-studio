## 二 简介

```
In January 2026, Defra published a national security assessment on global biodiversity loss and ecosystem collapse.

Key judgement four, analytical confidence High: every critical ecosystem is on a pathway to collapse — defined in the report as irreversible loss of function beyond repair.

Key judgement two: cascading risks are likely to include geopolitical instability, economic insecurity, conflict, migration and increased inter-state competition for resources.

The assessment applies the uncertainty frameworks used in intelligence assessments. It is fourteen pages long.

It was published following a freedom of information request. There was no launch and no press release.

To be fair, they did publish it. The PDF is still on gov.uk. Link in the pinned comment.

Every word here is from that document. What did you not read this year?
```

> 2026 年 1 月，Defra 发布了一份关于全球生物多样性丧失与生态系统崩溃的国家安全评估。
>
> 判断四，分析置信度 High：每一个关键生态系统都走在崩溃的路上——报告把 collapse 定义为「不可逆的、无法修复的功能丧失」。

> 判断二：连锁风险可能包括地缘政治不稳定、经济不安全、冲突、移民，以及国家间资源竞争加剧。
