## 3. TTS EN（只进引擎，无 ‖、无自造标记）

按 `‖` 切成 **16 段**，逐段生成。以下是各段的引擎输入文本：

```
01  Ninety-seven years.
02  That's how often a British road gets resurfaced.
03  If yours was done today, the next one is due in twenty one twenty-three.
04  Councils filled one point nine million potholes last year.
05  Five thousand two hundred a day.
06  The backlog still went up.
07  Eighteen point six billion pounds.
08  To be fair, budgets rose seventeen percent.
09  Conditions improved.
10  By three points.
11  So you pay road tax.
12  You pay for the pothole.
13  And you pay for your own suspension.
14  Three times.
15  One hole.
16  How long has yours been there?
```

**2026-09-09 从 9 段改成 16 段。** 原来只在段末的 `‖` 处切，稿面里另有 7 处 `‖`
落在段内（合计 3.2s），没有任何机制去实现 —— 只能指望引擎从句号里读出来，
而引擎给的句间停顿约 0.25–0.35s，比稿面要的 0.4–0.8s 短一截。Edge 试听版实听
下来，第 7 段「To be fair… Conditions improved. By three points.」那两处停顿明显
不够，反讽的落点是软的。

现在每一处 `‖` 都是一个段边界，15 处停顿全部由 `配音.mjs` 硬插静音实现。
**本地生成不计费，切多切少不花钱**，代价只是文件从 9 个变 16 个。

数字文本化已完成：`1.9 million` → one point nine million，`£18.6bn` → eighteen point six billion pounds，`17%` → seventeen percent，`2123` → twenty one twenty-three。

**本条不用任何方括号标签。** 语气全靠标点与停顿（方案 §4.5）。
