## 二 简介

```
On 2 January 2024, the government said the asylum backlog had been cleared.

The Home Office's own figures for that week: 98,599 cases awaiting an initial decision.

The pledge covered only claims filed before 28 June 2022 — "the appointed day" as defined in the Nationality and Borders Act 2022 commencement regulations. Of those, 4,537 were still waiting.

The UK Statistics Authority wrote to Parliament about how the claim had been presented.

To be fair: the backlog has since fallen by around three quarters. As of June 2026, 32,400 cases were awaiting an initial decision, down from a peak of 134,000.

No opinion is offered here. Every figure is from the government's own published records. Sources in the pinned comment.

Have you ever been counted as finished?
```

中文对照：

> 2024 年 1 月 2 日，政府表示庇护积压已经清空。
>
> 内政部自己那一周的数字：98,599 件待初次决定。
>
> 那个承诺只覆盖 2022 年 6 月 28 日之前提交的申请——即《2022 年国籍与边境法》生效法规里定义的「the appointed day」。其中还有 4,537 件在等。
>
> 英国统计监管局就该表述的呈现方式致函议会。
>
> 公平地说：积压此后降了约四分之三。截至 2026 年 6 月，待初次决定为 32,400 件，低于 134,000 件的峰值。
>
> 这里不提供任何观点。每一个数字都来自政府自己公布的记录。来源见置顶评论。
>
> 你有没有被算成「已办结」过？
