## 2. 稿面 EN（进字卡、字幕、核对）

```
The government said the backlog was cleared. ‖1.0

Ninety-eight thousand cases were waiting that day. ‖0.8

The pledge covered only claims filed before June twenty twenty-two. ‖0.6

Four thousand five hundred were still waiting. ‖0.8

The statistics regulator wrote about the wording. ‖0.8

To be fair, it has since fallen by three quarters. ‖0.6

Ninety-eight thousand. ‖1.0

Have you ever been counted as finished?
```

57 词（引擎实际念的分段词数）。停顿合计 5.6s。预估 30.2s，**实测 31.355s**（偏 3.8%）。
偏慢的原因是 `Ninety-eight thousand` 这种长数字读得慢 —— 数字多的稿子按 2.2 词/秒算更稳。

**第 7 行是落点**：把第 2 行那个数字原样再念一遍，不评论不解释。
数字重复本身就是判决 —— 这是《选题与锐度 v2 提案》里「说到哪儿停」那条。

`4,537` → four thousand five hundred、`2022-06` → June twenty twenty-two、`76%` → three quarters。
